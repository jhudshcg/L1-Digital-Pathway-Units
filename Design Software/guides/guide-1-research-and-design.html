STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE 1 OF 4

Research, Mood Board & Wireframing

Learn how to gather menu items, understand image copyright, and create your initial visual design before writing code.

1. Researching Your 10 Menu Items (AC 1.1, 1.2, 1.4)

Your canteen ordering system requires a balanced, realistic menu of at least 10 items.

Menu Requirements Checklist:
  • 4 Mains (e.g. burgers, wraps, pizza slices, jacket potatoes, salad bowls)
  • 3 Snacks / Desserts (e.g. crisps, brownies, fruit pots, flapjacks)
  • 3 Drinks (e.g. smoothies, milkshakes, sparkling water, tea/coffee)
  • At least 3 Healthy Items (tagged with healthpromo: true)
  • Allergy Information for each item (check for gluten, dairy, nuts, eggs, etc.)

2. Understanding Image Copyright & Sourcing (AC 1.3)

When creating digital designs, you must respect intellectual property and copyright laws. You cannot simply copy images from Google Search without permission.

Safe Royalty-Free Image Sources:
  • Unsplash (unsplash.com) — High quality, free to use.
  • Pexels (pexels.com) — Free stock food photography.
  • Pixabay (pixabay.com) — Free food graphics and photos.

Evidence Requirement: Keep a record of the URL and photographer/website name for every image in your Project Booklet table.

3. Creating Your Mood Board (AC 1.1, 1.5)

A mood board is a visual collage that establishes the tone, colour scheme, and vibe of your canteen app.

Steps to Build Your Mood Board:
  1. Choose a central theme (e.g. 50s American Diner, Neon Punk Bites, Fresh Garden Cafe, Retro School Tuckshop).
  2. Select 3–4 main theme colours (e.g. Primary Red, Warm Yellow, Off-White, Charcoal).
  3. Collect 4–6 inspiration images of logos, food packaging, and typography that match your vibe.
  4. Arrange them on a digital canvas using provector.app or a graphics tool.
  5. Export your mood board image (PNG/JPG) and paste it into Section 2 of your Project Booklet.

4. Wireframing Your User Interface (AC 1.2, 2.1)

A wireframe is a simplified visual blueprint showing where elements will sit on the screen before you style them.

UI Area What to Include in Your Wireframe
Header App Title, Subtitle/Tagline, Theme Logo
Filter Bar Category Dropdown (All, Mains, Snacks, Drinks) & Student Voucher Checkbox
Menu Area Grid or list of food cards (Image, Title, Price, Description, Allergen badge, '+ Add' button)
Tray / Basket List of added items, Subtotal, Discount row, Total to pay, Feedback alert banner, Clear & Order buttons
