🥗 Daily Nutrition & Feeding Routine ▼

Adult dogs require 2 balanced meals per day tailored to their weight and activity level. Always keep fresh water accessible throughout the day.

🎾 Exercise, Stimulation & Play ▼

Aim for at least 60 to 90 minutes of active exercise daily. Puzzle toys, scent trails, and agility games provide great mental stimulation.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Maintenance ▼

Brush their double coat 2–3 times a week to reduce shedding and prevent mats. Regular nail trimming and ear checks keep them comfortable.

💡 3 Amazing Fun Facts ▼
  • Soft Mouth: They can hold a raw egg in their mouth without breaking it!
  • Water Lovers: They have webbed toes and a water-repellent outer coat.
  • Service Heroes: They are one of the most common breeds used for search-and-rescue and guide dog work.
