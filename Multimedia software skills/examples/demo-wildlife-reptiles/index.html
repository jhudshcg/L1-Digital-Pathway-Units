Meet the Bearded Dragon

Bearded dragons are friendly lizards that come from the dry deserts and bushlands of Australia. They get their name from the spiky skin under their chin, which puffs out when they feel excited or scared.

Tank Setup & Daily Care

Click each topic below to see simple tips for keeping a happy dragon

☀️ Heating & Warm Basking Spot ▼

Dragons need a hot basking spot (about 40°C) at one end of the tank, and a cooler spot (about 25°C) at the other end so they can cool down when needed.

🥗 Food, Water & Calcium Powder ▼

Feed young dragons insects every day, and give adult dragons bowls of fresh salad. Lightly dust their food with calcium powder to keep their bones strong.

🪵 Branches, Rocks & Climbing ▼

Add strong wooden branches and flat rocks for climbing and resting. Keep a shallow water bowl clean and fresh every day.
