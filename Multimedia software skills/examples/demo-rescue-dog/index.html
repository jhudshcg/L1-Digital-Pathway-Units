Click to reveal details about keeping a beagle healthy and happy

🦴 Diet & Avoiding Overeating ▼

Beagles have a voracious appetite and will happily eat anything in sight! We use slow feeder bowls and measure kibble portions twice daily.

🐕 Scent Games & Exercise Needs ▼

Mental stimulation is just as important as physical walks. Hiding kibble around the house in snuffle mats keeps his sharp hound brain exercised.

📢 The Famous Beagle 'Barroo' Howl ▼

Beagles don't just bark—they make a musical howling sound called a 'barroo' whenever they spot a squirrel or hear an emergency siren!
