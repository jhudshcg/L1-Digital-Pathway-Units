2. Copyright, Licensing & Sourcing (AC 1.4)

You cannot just grab random copyrighted photos from Google Images. You must source royalty-free assets or use your own original photos/videos:

  • Unsplash (unsplash.com): High-quality, free-to-use photography.
  • Pexels (pexels.com): Great royalty-free stock photos and short video clips.
  • Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org): Public domain wildlife and nature photos.
  • Own Smartphone Footage: Record your own pet or webcam video introduction!

3. Cropping & Sizing Images for the Web (AC 3.1 & 3.2)

Raw photos from cameras or Unsplash can be huge (5MB+ and 4000px wide). This will slow down your webpage!

How to Crop & Resize in Microsoft Photos / Paint:

  1. Open your downloaded image in Photos or Paint on Windows.
  2. Click Crop and set the aspect ratio (e.g. 16:9 widescreen or 4:3).
  3. Click Resize / Resize Image and set the width to 800 pixels.
  4. Save/Export as .jpg or .webp into your project's images/ folder.

4. Trimming Videos with Microsoft Clipchamp (AC 3.1 & 3.2)

Videos on web pages should be short (15–45 seconds) so they load instantly without lagging.

Trimming Video in MS Clipchamp:

  1. Open Microsoft Clipchamp on Windows (search in Start menu) or use your phone's video editor.
  2. Import your video clip into the timeline.
  3. Drag the left and right trim handles to cut out dead time at the start and end.
  4. Optional: Add a simple text title card (e.g. "Barnaby's Fetch Time") or lower-third caption.
  5. Click Export and choose 720p or 1080p MP4.
  6. Save the exported video directly into your videos/ folder!
