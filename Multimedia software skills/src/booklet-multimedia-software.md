## Assignment Brief & Requirements

### Project Title
**Interactive Pet & Animal Showcase Webpage**

### Unit Aim
This unit enables you to plan, source, edit, format, and combine text, images, and video into an interactive, responsive multimedia webpage, and present your finished product with playback controls across digital devices.

### Project Brief & Scenario
You have been commissioned to create an entertaining and informative single-page **Pet or Wildlife Showcase Webpage** about a pet or animal of your choice (such as a domestic pet, rescue animal, reptile, or wildlife species).

Your completed multimedia webpage must include:
1. **Interactive Responsive Webpage:** 1 HTML file (`index.html`) and 1 stylesheet (`style.css`) using responsive layouts and accessible styling.
2. **Photo Gallery (Minimum 4 Images):** Researched, cropped, and web-optimized photos showing your animal in different contexts (e.g. habitat, feeding, action, close-up).
3. **Video Showcase (Minimum 2 Videos):** Embedded videos with native playback controls (`play`, `pause`, `volume`, `fullscreen`), including at least one video you have trimmed or edited.
4. **Care Guide & Facts:** Structured text headings, bullet points, and an interactive click-to-reveal accordion.
5. **Local Media Storage & Version Control:** All image and video files stored locally in organized folders (`images/`, `videos/`) with regular Git commits tracking your progress.

*(Note: All images, sound clips, and videos must be college-appropriate and family-friendly).*

### Useful Links & Starter Resources
- **Starter Template Code:** Located in `l1-multimedia-software-student/template/` (`index.html`, `style.css`, `images/`, `videos/`)
- **Exemplar Demo Projects:** Located in `l1-multimedia-software-student/examples/` (`demo-rescue-dog`, `demo-wildlife-reptiles`)
- **Student HTML Guides:** Located in `l1-multimedia-software-student/guides/` (`git-and-testing-guide.html`, `guide-1-research-and-design.html`, `guide-2-customising-styles.html`)
- **Royalty-Free Media Sources:** `https://unsplash.com`, `https://pexels.com`, `https://commons.wikimedia.org`
- **Video & Image Tools:** Microsoft Clipchamp, Microsoft Photos / Paint, Squoosh (`https://squoosh.app`)
