## 1. Introduction to the Overworld

Minecraft is a 3D sandbox video game where players explore an infinite, procedurally generated blocky terrain, discover raw materials, craft tools, and build structures.

### Game Modes & Core Mechanics
Players can choose between **Survival Mode**, where resources must be gathered and health must be managed, and **Creative Mode**, which provides unlimited building blocks and flight abilities.

::: {.layout-textbox height="3cm" title="Pro Tip Callout:"}
**Explorer Tip:** Always craft a bed before nightfall on your first day to set your respawn point and skip the dangerous night cycle!
:::

## 3. Best Practices & Useful Links

To advance further into the game, explore the official resources below:

- **Official Wiki:** Visit the [Minecraft Official Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki) for full crafting recipes.
- **Crafting Guide:** Use in-game recipe books to quickly discover new items.

### Key Rules for Safe Mining:
1. Never dig straight down (you might fall into lava).
2. Always carry a bucket of water to turn lava pools into obsidian.
3. Place torches on the right wall so you can follow torches on your left to exit caves.
