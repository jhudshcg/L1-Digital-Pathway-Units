## Assignment Brief & Requirements

### Project Title
**Special Interest Report & Multi-Page Guide (5+ Pages)**

### Unit Aim
This unit enables you to enter, edit, structure, format, and present text and combined media within professional multi-page word processing documents using Microsoft Word.

### Project Brief & Scenario
You have been asked to create a high-quality, structured multi-page document (minimum 5 pages) about a **favourite interest or topic** (such as a video game, sport, musical artist, hobby, film/TV series, automotive, or book series).

Your completed Word document must include:
1. **Cover Page:** Title, subtitle, author details, and a styled feature image.
2. **Table of Contents (Page 2):** An automated Table of Contents linked to your heading styles.
3. **Content Pages (Pages 3, 4, 5+):** Well-structured sections with consistent paragraph styling, bullet/numbered lists, and external hyperlinks.
4. **Structured Table:** At least one styled table displaying structured facts, stats, or schedule data.
5. **Callout Text Box:** At least one styled text box highlighting an important tip, quote, or key fact.
6. **Consistent Page Setup:** Page numbers (`Page X of Y`), running headers on Page 2+, and clean section breaks.
